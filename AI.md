# History and Context: SpeedCrunch has fallen

The contributor to the calculator this whole project was originally "based on" (at least the idea) has recently changed from a human to *claude code*... To be fair, not actually. Claude only has a small number of contributions, but nonetheless the amount of contributions has recently sky-rocketed; I would guess the maintainer uses at least some amount of AI as a tool. And I can't blame him, everyone does it at the moment, but I prefer to use software still written by humans.
For me, that results in my inability to use said software; I might seem old-fashioned or stupid, but I hate AI; 
The high cost for the environment, the injustice of data it was trained on, the stupid hype bound to burst at some point; just to mention a few. 
Therefore, I prompty decided to not use SpeedCrunch and switch completely to my own calculator...
Now, I might finally need to actually think of how to package and distribute this very software I originally developed just for fun, even if still just for me. 
I am genuinely glad I "finished" development rather parallel to SpeedCrunch jumping on the AI hype train. I must admit, though, this codebase does have one single part left, which, too, is written by an AI. And it does not have a very high priority for me to fix in a timely manner. However it's a part responsible only for a very slim part of the program (displaying numbers with a nice amount of digits) and the extend of the code is only 65 lines of code, which were all manually checked by me. I will keep it that way.
